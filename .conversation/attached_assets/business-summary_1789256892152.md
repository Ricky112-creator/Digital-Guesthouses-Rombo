# Digital Guesthouses Rombo — Business Summary

## Overview
Digital Guesthouses Rombo is a hospitality business operating in **Rombo, Kenya**, near the border with Tanzania and close to **Amboseli National Park**. The property sits with views of **Mount Kilimanjaro**. The brand operates under the umbrella name "Digital Guesthouses Rombo" and consists of four distinct but adjacent service offerings.

---

## Properties & Services

### 1. Mt. Betheli Digital Guesthouse (B&B)
- **Type:** Premium bed-and-breakfast lodging
- **Positioning:** Affordable luxury for couples, solo travelers, and those seeking comfort with a view
- **Price Range:** KES 1,500 – 3,500 per night
- **Key Features:**
  - Private premium single rooms
  - Complimentary breakfast included
  - Direct Mount Kilimanjaro views from rooms
  - On-site dining at Winners Restaurant
  - 24/7 security
  - Free Wi-Fi
  - Hot showers
- **Ideal For:** Couples, solo travelers, safari-goers heading to Amboseli
- **Location:** Rombo, ~30 meters from Digital Guesthouse

### 2. Digital Guesthouse
- **Type:** Budget-friendly family lodging
- **Positioning:** Clean, safe, affordable rooms for families and groups
- **Price Range:** KES 600 – 1,200 per night
- **Key Features:**
  - Self-contained family rooms (single & two-bed)
  - Spacious accommodations for groups
  - 30 meters from Winners Restaurant
  - 24/7 security
  - Free Wi-Fi
  - Hot showers
- **Ideal For:** Families, friend groups, budget travelers who refuse to compromise on cleanliness or safety

### 3. Winners Restaurant
- **Type:** On-site restaurant serving local Kenyan cuisine
- **Positioning:** Convenient dining for guests of both properties, plus room service
- **Key Features:**
  - Local Kenyan cuisine
  - Room service available to both guesthouses
  - On-site at Mt. Betheli location
  - Breakfast included for Mt. Betheli guests
- **Menu:** Kenyan staples, breakfast options, meals for travelers and safari groups

### 4. Conference Hall
- **Type:** Event and meeting venue
- **Positioning:** Business meetings, training sessions, small conferences
- **Capacity:** 30 seats
- **Location:** At Mt. Betheli property
- **Key Features:**
  - Hourly rental rates available
  - Integrated catering from Winners Restaurant
  - Time-slot bookings
  - Suitable for corporate retreats, workshops, and training

## Booking & Payment Flow

1. Guest browses rooms/services on the website
2. Clicks "Book Now on WhatsApp" button (pre-filled message)
3. WhatsApp chat opens with pre-populated booking request
4. Staff and guest confirm dates, room type, and pricing via chat
5. Booking is confirmed manually by the business

**Note:** The site previously had a simulated M-Pesa payment flow with PDF receipt generation. This was removed for security reasons. All payments are now handled directly via WhatsApp conversation.
